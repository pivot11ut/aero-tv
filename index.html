SAMSUNG TV INSTALLATION GUIDE

How to Install & Replace Samsung Smart Hub UI

1

Host Locally or Access This Dashboard

You can run this lightweight web app on your home Wi-Fi network (on a Mac, PC, Raspberry Pi, or NAS) with a single command:

npm run serve   # or: python3 -m http.server 8080

Alternatively, host it for free on GitHub Pages, Vercel, or Cloudflare Pages.

2

Open on Samsung TV Web Browser

On your Samsung TV remote, open the built-in "Internet" app (Web Browser). Type your local IP or hosted URL (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:8080).

3

Add Bookmark & Enable Fullscreen Mode

Press the star icon (⭐) in the top-right of Samsung Internet to bookmark it, and click Fullscreen. The Samsung TV browser remembers fullscreen state!

1

Enable Developer Mode on Your Samsung TV

On your Samsung TV remote, open Apps. On the remote keypad, press 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 in sequence. A Developer Mode popup will appear. Switch it to ON, enter your computer's local IP address, and restart your TV (hold remote Power button for 5 seconds until the TV logo appears).

2

Build the Tizen .wgt Package

In this project directory, run our automated build script:

./scripts/build-tizen.sh

This creates dist/AeroTV.wgt ready for installation.

3

Install via Samsung Debug Bridge (SDB) or Tizen Studio

Connect to your TV from your computer terminal and install:

sdb connect [TV-IP-ADDRESS]
sdb install dist/AeroTV.wgt

Aero TV will now appear in your TV's Apps row as a native Tizen app!

1

Why Samsung TVs Show Ads

Samsung Smart Hub downloads ad banners and video recommendations from dedicated Samsung ad telemetry servers. You can block them completely without rooting the TV!

2

Domains to Block (in Pi-hole, AdGuard Home, or Router DNS)

Add these exact domains to your blacklist:

samsungads.com
config.samsungcloudcdn.com
gpm.samsungqbe.com
log-config.samsungcloudcdn.com
smetrics.samsung.com
noticecdn.samsungcloudcdn.com
oempprod.samsungcloudcdn.com
3

Disable "Samsung TV Plus" in TV Settings

Go to Settings ➔ General ➔ Smart Features ➔ Autorun Samsung TV Plus and turn it OFF. Also turn off "Terms & Privacy ➔ Interest-Based Advertisements".

1

Bypass the Stock Samsung Smart Hub on Boot

Samsung Tizen has a built-in feature that can completely bypass the noisy default Smart Hub and boot directly into your last opened app or source!

2

TV Settings Configuration

Go to TV: Settings ➔ General ➔ Smart Features:

  • Set "Autorun Smart Hub" ➔ OFF
  • Set "Autorun Last App" ➔ ON

Now, whenever you turn your TV on with the power button, it will boot directly into Aero TV without showing the Samsung home screen or ads!
